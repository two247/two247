Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 01 Private Airport Sedan

Arrival in Dubai & Evening Marina Dhow Cruise

Upon landing at Dubai International Airport (DXB), meet your private chauffeur and transfer to your selected 4-star or 5-star hotel. In the evening, step aboard a traditional wooden dhow in Dubai Marina for a 2-hour cruise with skyline illuminations, live Tanoura dance, and an international buffet dinner.

Day 02 Full Day Tour

Half-Day Panoramic City Tour & Burj Khalifa Sky

Discover modern and historic Dubai: pass Jumeirah Mosque, photo stop at Burj Al Arab, explore the Dubai Frame, and visit The Dubai Mall. Ascend the world's tallest tower to the 124th & 125th observation deck for 360-degree views before watching the evening Dubai Fountain show.

Day 03 4x4 Desert Safari

Morning at Leisure & Premium Red Dune Safari

Enjoy your morning relaxing by the hotel pool or shopping. At 2:30 PM, your 4x4 Land Cruiser arrives for an exhilarating desert safari across Lahbab red dunes. Experience sandboarding, camel rides, henna painting, and a five-star Arabic BBQ dinner under the desert stars with live fire shows.

Day 04 Optional Activities

Miracle Garden, Global Village or Shopping Spree

Visit the spectacular Dubai Miracle Garden with over 150 million blooming flowers, followed by an evening at Global Village to sample authentic street foods, handicrafts, and cultural pavilions from over 75 countries.

Day 05 Departure Transfer

Souk Gold & Spice Shopping & Departure

Enjoy breakfast at your hotel. Explore the traditional Deira Gold & Spice Souks for saffron, perfumes, and authentic souvenirs before your private transfer to DXB Airport for your journey home.
