A standard 5 to 7-day Thailand holiday package typically costs between ₹35,000 to ₹75,000 ($420 – $900) per person. Budget travelers spend around 1,500 – 2,500 THB/day, mid-range travelers spend 3,500 – 5,500 THB/day, and luxury vacations in Phuket or Koh Samui range from 8,000 to 20,000+ THB/day.
Yes, legally under Thai Immigration regulations. When entering on a standard Tourist Visa or Visa Exemption, tourists must have proof of at least 20,000 THB in cash (or foreign currency equivalent) per person, or 40,000 THB per family. For Visa on Arrival (VoA), the requirement is 10,000 THB per person (20,000 THB per family). While immigration officers perform random spot-checks, carrying hard cash or an active bank statement is strongly recommended to guarantee smooth entry.
