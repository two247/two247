Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • TWO247

At TWO247 — The White Owl Holidays, we are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our website visitors and clients. This Privacy Policy details how your personal information is gathered, utilized, and safeguarded when submitting travel requests or booking holiday packages.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or request custom travel itineraries, we may collect:

  • Personal Contact Data: Name, phone/WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Travel Requirements: Destination preferences, approximate dates, group composition, budget tier, and custom requests.
  • Booking Information: Passport data and government IDs necessary solely for hotel registration and airline ticket issuance.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your details are utilized exclusively to:

  • Create and deliver customized travel itineraries and price quotations.
  • Facilitate real-time on-trip coordination via WhatsApp or telephone during your travels.
  • Process reservations with vetted hotel partners, transport providers, and tour suppliers.

3. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, lease, or monetize your contact information. Relevant details are shared only with registered hotel and transport providers strictly as required to fulfill your confirmed travel itinerary.

4. Data Security

We apply strict administrative and technical safeguards to keep personal data confidential. No private access keys or third-party tracking scripts compromise your browsing privacy.

5. Contact Our Privacy Team

If you have questions regarding your data, contact our operations team at travel@two247holidays.com or visit our registered office.
