Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • TWO247 — The White Owl Holidays

We understand that unexpected life events can necessitate changing your holiday schedule. TWO247 — The White Owl Holidays maintains transparent cancellation and refund guidelines aimed at protecting your investments while respecting contracted resort and airline policies.

1. Standard Cancellation Timeline

Notice Received Prior to Departure Applicable Retention / Cancellation Fee
30 Days or More Standard service charge / non-refundable flight actuals only
15 to 29 Days 25% of total package cost + non-refundable hotel actuals
7 to 14 Days 50% of total package cost
Less than 7 Days / No-Show 100% of package cost (subject to hotel peak terms)

2. Peak Season & Island Atoll Stays

Certain specialized accommodations (such as private Maldives water villas or Swiss peak Christmas chalets) enforce strict non-refundable terms dictated directly by resort management. These terms are explicitly detailed on your voucher prior to payment.

3. Date Rescheduling & Trip Credits

Whenever possible, we advocate with resorts and airlines to convert cancelled departures into travel credits valid for future travel rather than forfeiting sums.

4. Refund Processing

Eligible refunds are reviewed and processed within 7 to 10 business days directly to the original payment source. For assistance, reach our desk at info@two247.in.
