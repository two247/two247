A standard 5 to 7-day Thailand holiday package typically costs between ₹35,000 to ₹75,000 ($420 – $900) per person. On average:
  • Budget traveler: 1,500 – 2,500 THB/day (hostels, street food, BTS trains).
  • Mid-range traveler: 3,500 – 5,500 THB/day (4-star beachfront hotels, island ferries, cafe dining).
  • Luxury traveler: 8,000 – 20,000+ THB/day (5-star private pool villas in Phuket/Samui, private catamarans).

Yes, legally under Thai Immigration regulations. When entering on a standard Tourist Visa or 60-day Visa Exemption, tourists must have proof of at least 20,000 THB in cash (or equivalent in USD/INR/EUR) per individual traveler, or 40,000 THB per family. For Visa on Arrival (VoA), the requirement is 10,000 THB per person (20,000 THB per family). While immigration officers perform random spot-checks, carrying hard cash or an active bank statement is strongly recommended to guarantee smooth entry.
Yes, 10,000 THB (approx. ₹23,500 / $280) is enough for pocket spending if your hotels, flights, and major tours are already prepaid through your TWO247 itinerary. 10,000 THB gives you roughly 1,420 THB/day, which comfortably covers delicious street food meals (60 – 120 THB each), local 7-Eleven snacks, BTS/MRT train rides, and temple tickets. If you plan to indulge in scuba diving, private longtail boats, fine-dining rooftops, or vibrant nightlife, budgeting 18,000 – 25,000 THB is recommended.
Yes, Thailand is exceptionally safe for tourists and ranks among the most peaceful, hospitable vacation spots in Asia. Major corridors including Bangkok, Phuket, Krabi, Koh Samui, and Chiang Mai welcome millions of tourists annually with active Tourist Police (Emergency Hotline 1155). Standard travel safety guidelines:
  • Use metered taxis or the Grab ride-hailing app rather than unmetered cabs.
  • Drink sealed bottled water and dine at busy, hygienic food stalls.
